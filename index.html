Can't Think

Some days the noise wins.

Chaos, hate, ignorance, taught bias, racism, greed — there's so much of it that just sitting still to think feels impossible. And if you do dare to dwell on it, it's hard to know where to even begin making sense of it, let alone becoming a catalyst for change.

This is a place to start anyway.

I have no idea what the answers are; I have ideas! If you want to hang out and see what we can figure out this is the place for you!
