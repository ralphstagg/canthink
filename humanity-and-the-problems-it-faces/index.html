Humanity and the Problems It Faces…

Crime, cruelty, poverty, the way we treat each other — the hard stuff gets talked about in slogans more often than in honest numbers or honest reckoning. These pages try to do the opposite: lay out what’s actually broken, what the evidence shows about why, and what it might take for humanity to evolve past it.

Each page follows the same shape: the headline numbers first, then the trends behind them, then an honest look at who the data covers well, who it doesn’t, and what it would take to do better — with sources for every figure.
