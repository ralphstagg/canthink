Words & Society

A lot of arguments are really arguments about words. One side hears an insult, the other hears a description, and nobody stops to ask where the word came from or what it used to mean. These pages trace the loaded language of everyday politics back to its roots — carefully, with sources, and without taking sides.

Each page follows the same shape: the short answer first, then the long history, then an honest accounting of how different people use the word today — including the people who use it as an insult.
